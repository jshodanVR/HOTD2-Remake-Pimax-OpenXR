# Changelog

## v1.0.0-beta.1

First public Pimax/OpenXR preset based on Astien's full FPS_FIX mod distribution.

- OpenXR selected in the included VR configuration.
- Weapon pitch raised 27 degrees; pistol shifted 4 cm forward and 4 cm upward.
- Left-stick click advances to the next weapon once per press.
- All weapons available through the companion plugin.
- Finite magazines: pistol base capacity x3 plus ordinary bonuses; other weapons x2.
- Ammunition display follows the modified capacity.
- Original FPS fix, dependencies, artwork, credits and licenses retained.
- Companion plugins and calibration build tool supplied as source.
